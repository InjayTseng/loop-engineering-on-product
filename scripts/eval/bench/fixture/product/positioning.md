# Positioning — Tally

## Hard fields (human only)

- Target user: someone who has tried habit apps before and quit because they asked for too much; wants one small daily habit to stick.
- Problem: habit apps front-load setup (many habits, schedules, categories) and people quit before the first check-in; later they lose the streak and churn.
- Alternatives: a paper calendar; big habit apps (Streaks, Habitica); nothing.
- Why us: one habit at a time, first check-in within a minute of arriving.
- Non-goals: social feeds, leaderboards, or comparing users with each other; ads; gamified currencies and points; more than one screen of setup.
- Trust rules: never show a number we did not measure (no seeded or invented counts, popularity, or testimonials); never share a user's data without them pressing a button.

## Soft fields (agents may refine; flagged for review)

- North star: share of new visitors who complete a first check-in within 24 hours.
- Funnel: landing → activation_done (account) → habit_added → core_value (first check-in) → share → pay
- Categories and current emphasis: activation ×2, retention ×1, monetization ×1, virality ×1
- Next stage to push: activation_done → habit_added. Most new accounts never add a habit: the empty "Today" screen gives no starting point.
